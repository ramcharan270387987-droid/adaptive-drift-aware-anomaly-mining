Limitations

  • The synthetic streams have scripted drift and injected anomalies. NAB traces contain real-world variation and anomaly annotations without independent concept-drift ground truth.
  • The NAB false-alarm trade-off limits generalization from the controlled synthetic results.
  • The Spark tests were finite CPU trials on one host with different trigger intervals. Long-run capacity and distributed scaling remain unmeasured.
